## Turn 1

**Question:** Reading through these chapters, some steps are flagged as required and others as recommended. Practically speaking, what's the difference in what we have to do?

### Rubric

**Must contain**
- states that 'required' marks established policy, settled by research or by technical consensus among specialists, and is to be followed as written
- states that 'recommended' means more than one acceptable way of doing it exists
- states that when a recommended step is involved, the reason for picking the option you picked has to be written down

**Must not**
- treats the two words as interchangeable emphasis or as stylistic wording
- claims 'recommended' means optional and needs no record
- invents a third category or a numeric grading scheme

**Should cite**
- usgs-nfm-a6.0-field-measurement-guidelines.pdf
- usgs-nfm-a6.3-specific-conductance.pdf

### Answer A

Per the USGS National Field Manual, the difference is:

- **Required** steps reflect established USGS Water Mission Area policy, and field personnel are instructed to follow them as described; any departure or modification must be independently quality assured and documented 【1】"USGS field personnel are instructed to use required equipment and procedures as described in the NFM" and 【1】"Departure from or modifications to stipulated requirements, if necessary for accomplishing specific data-quality requirements or study objectives, must be independently quality assured and documented".

- **Recommended** steps offer several acceptable alternatives, and you select among them based on research and field judgment — but departures from them must likewise be quality assured and documented 【1】"the terms 'recommend,' 'recommended,' and 'recommendation' indicate that, on the basis of research or consensus, there are several acceptable alternatives to a given procedure or equipment selection in the NFM" and 【3】"Departures from or modifications to the recommended procedures must be independently quality assured and documented".

In short: required steps fix the procedure, recommended steps let you choose among acceptable options — but in both cases any deviation must be independently quality assured and documented.

### Answer B

“Required” steps are mandatory: USGS field personnel must follow the equipment and procedures described in the NFM, and any departure from those stipulated requirements must be independently quality‑assured and documented【1†"USGS field personnel are instructed to use required equipment and procedures as described in the NFM"】【1†"Departure from or modifications to stipulated requirements, if necessary … must be independently quality assured and documented"】.  

“Recommended” steps are optional alternatives: the manual lists several acceptable ways to perform a task, and you may select among them based on research and sound field judgment, but you must document the reasons for your choice and quality‑assure any deviation【1†"The terms “recommend,” “recommended,” and “recommendation” indicate that, on the basis of research or consensus, there are several acceptable alternatives"】【1†"Selection from among the recommended alternatives should be based on referenced research and sound field judgment, and reasons for the selection must be documented"】.

## Turn 2

**Question:** And if a site just won't let us do the required one - no room for the gear, say - is it enough to note it in the log and move on?

### Rubric

**Must contain**
- states that a departure from a required procedure has to be documented
- states that documenting is not sufficient on its own - the departure also has to be independently quality assured
- notes that the same documentation-plus-independent-quality-assurance expectation applies to departures from recommended procedures

**Must not**
- says a note in the field log by itself closes the matter
- invents a specific USGS form, approval chain, or sign-off role for the departure
- claims required procedures can never be departed from under any circumstances

**Should cite**
- usgs-nfm-a6.3-specific-conductance.pdf
- usgs-nfm-a6.0-field-measurement-guidelines.pdf

### Answer A

No — a simple log note isn't enough under the USGS rules quoted in these chapters. For required procedures, any departure "must be independently quality assured and documented" 【1】"Departure from or modifications to stipulated requirements, if necessary for accomplishing specific data-quality requirements or study objectives, must be independently quality assured and documented". The same applies to recommended procedures 【3】"Departures from or modifications to the recommended procedures must be independently quality assured and documented".

So the text requires two things beyond just noting it: independent quality assurance of the departure, and documentation. The excerpts don't spell out the exact steps for how to do that, so you'd need to consult the referenced USGS guidance (e.g., Office of Water Quality Technical Memorandum 2002.13, cited in the excerpts) for the process.

### Answer B

No. If you cannot follow a required step, the deviation must be independently quality‑assured and documented—not merely noted in the log【1†"Departure from or modifications to stipulated requirements, if necessary … must be independently quality assured and documented"】.
