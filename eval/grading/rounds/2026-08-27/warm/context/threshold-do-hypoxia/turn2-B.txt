2.1 Equipment Transport
Multiparameter instruments have become more robust
with time, but the sensors can easily be damaged if handled
improperly. Some sensors require storage and transporta-
tion in a wet/moist environment, whereas others do not,
check the manufacturer’s recommendations. Sondes should
not be exposed to extreme heat or cold. Transport in a case
that is designed to protect the equipment following the
manufacturer’s recommendations is best. For transportation
and storage, use a padded case that protects the sonde from
shock. The padding should be of a type that will not absorb
water. Hard-sided cases should be equipped with a vent so
that internal pressure can equalize with that of the atmosphere.
Do not expose the case to direct sunlight in order to avoid
solar heating of the equipment inside.
