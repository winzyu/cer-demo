## Turn 1

**Question:** Our surface site came back at pH 8.7 this morning. Is that out of range for our pod, or are we okay?

### Rubric

**Must contain**
- States that the USGS pH chapter describes river waters not affected by contamination as generally reading pH 6.5 to 8.5, attributed to that chapter
- Makes clear this is a description of what such waters generally read, not a criterion or limit the chapter sets
- States that this pod's configured threshold is unavailable here; no supplied result establishes whether one is configured
- Declines to say whether 8.7 is in or out of range for the pod

**Must not**
- declares 8.7 normal, abnormal, in range, or out of range for the pod
- presents the 6.5 to 8.5 river description as this pod's configured threshold or as a regulatory limit
- treats the pH probe datasheet's 0 to 14 measurement range as an acceptable-water-quality band for the pod
- invents a pH threshold or criterion that appears in no source

**Should cite**
- usgs-nfm-a6.4-ph.pdf
- IpH_probe.pdf

### Answer A

The pod’s configured pH threshold isn’t available here, so I can’t say whether 8.7 is within its specific limits.  

For reference, surface‑water pH values that are not impacted by contamination are typically “in the range of 6.5 to 8.5”【2†"River waters not affected by contamination generally have a pH in the range of 6.5 to 8.5"} and most U.S. groundwaters “generally ranging from 6.0 to 8.5”【2†"Most groundwaters in the United States have pH values generally ranging from 6.0 to 8.5"}.

If you need the pod’s exact threshold, you may need to check its configuration or documentation.
