## Interpretation and findings

Counts are per fixture-claim pair, so a claim reused by another fixture is counted again.
A claim listed under both turns contributes once to the claims column and once to the both column.
An uncovered cell gives the count, followed by the 1-based rubric indexes in parentheses when nonzero.
All 340 resolving fixture-claim pairs are assigned; the only nonresolving claim-shaped token is `in-situ-vs-25`, which is excluded.
There are 287 distinct claim ids across these pairs.
The four refusal fixtures are excluded.

An assignment can support a component of a compound rubric point without establishing the entire point.
`uncovered` lists only points with no supporting assigned claim, as requested; an empty list is not a finding that every detail is supported.
Explicit evidence does not widen a claim's content: a table pointer is not treated as a claim containing all of that table's values, and a quote relevant only to the sibling turn does not justify assignment to the current turn.

Notable partial gaps remain in the frozen claim sets:

- `crossdoc-soft-water-ph-wont-settle`, turn 2 point 3: the assigned form claim supplies the comparison's A6.8 side but not A6.4's 0.05 pH final-buffer check.
- `probecal-end-of-day-check`, turn 2 point 5: assigned claims supply EPA's 0.5 mg/L default and project-criteria qualification but not USGS's 0.2 mg/L verification value.
- `crossdoc-how-steady-before-i-write-it-down`, turn 2 point 1: claims supply the general criteria and the cross-reference to similar multiparameter criteria, but no named claim enumerates the multiparameter table's numerical values for the full comparison.
- `crossdoc-orp-sliding-do-steady`, turn 2 point 1: the optical-DO numerical tolerance is supported, but the five-reading count is absent from the named claims.
- `crossdoc-sonde-sensor-order`, turn 1 point 3: the general order and parameter-chapter precedence are supported, but the two-point DO exception itself is not among this fixture's named claims.
- `definitional-what-per-centimetre-means`, turn 1 point 2: geometry normalization is supported, but the explicit electrode-spacing/area cell-constant formula is not stated in the named claim.

Some findings concern absent deployment information rather than missing manual passages.
The precedence fixtures' claims cannot establish whether any pod threshold is configured, and the turbidity precedence fixture's sole claim does not establish the deployment's relative, uncalibrated interpretation.
These points remain uncovered rather than being inferred from generic manual ranges.
